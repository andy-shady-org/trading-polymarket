# Release record – 0.5.0 (2026-11-07)

Research commands, all record-only. FEAT-168: `sports-gaps` records tennis
and esports matches listed on both Polymarket and Kalshi (both venues' best
prices and sizes for each side every 2 minutes, from listing to 3 h after
the start, with each venue's listing time) and `sports-gaps-report` finds
the gaps after Polymarket's fee and gives the decision of the run it serves,
`sports-1` ([`../runs/sports-1.md`](../runs/sports-1.md)). Also released
here, built since 0.4.0: `lane-rows` (FEAT-149), `rewards-scan`
(FEAT-153) and `updown-backtest --rule fade` (FEAT-103, NO-GO,
[`../RESULTS.md`](../RESULTS.md)). No lane, strategy, setting or fill
changes; no run's result changes. Needs trading-core 0.6.0 or later.

Cut on 2026-11-07 at the operator's request so that `sports-1` runs from a
release copy, ahead of the plan to release with trading-core `v0.8.0`:
`lane-rows` works on `v0.7.0`, and the price-dependent fee of
`trading-core-lanes sweep` comes with `v0.8.0`.
